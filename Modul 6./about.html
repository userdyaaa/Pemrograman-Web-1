The Story Behind the Lens

Hello! I am Nadya, an award-winning photographer and cinematographer based in the beautiful landscapes of Indonesia. My passion is capturing the raw, authentic moments of life and nature, transforming fleeting seconds into timeless stories.

With over a decade of experience, my work has taken me around the globe. Whether it is a quiet portrait or a breathtaking landscape, I strive to find the extraordinary in the everyday.
